# Visual critique (boss review of docs/lifesim/shots/audit, 2026-10-01)

Overall: coherent and warm, but it reads as "competent asset-pack game", not "amazing". The biggest problems are ground noise, repetition/emptiness, a few broken-looking buildings, UI weight, and style clashes in the out-of-world screens.

A. Ground (biggest impact)
- The calçada wave is a noisy 1-px knit/dither texture that buzzes at every zoom and dominates every outdoor shot. A real SP/Copacabana wave is bold, smooth, 2-tone bands. Needs a clean, calmer, larger-scale pattern.
- The SP-map mosaic decals read as white blobs on black squares. They're unrecognizable and repeated 6+ times. Make them fewer, bigger and clearer, or replace them.
- Grass is flat and uniform with hard straight edges against paving: no curbs, no tonal variation, no worn paths.
- Streets have no curbs (meio-fio), no parked cars, no wear or patches, and odd "BUS" lettering.

B. Composition / variety
- The praça is a 4-way symmetric template: same lamp + bench + mosaic + ipê in every quadrant. There's no focal point except the fountain.
- Every tree is the same yellow ipê blob. Missing: ipê roxo/branco, green shade trees, a palm (jerivá), tree pits on sidewalks.
- Big empty grass areas (praça quadrants, around the feira, west).
- Missing life props that scream "praça in SP": a coreto (bandstand), mesas de dominó/xadrez with seniors, a pipoqueiro cart, a coconut-water cart, a small playground, a bust/statue, a newspaper/flower vendor, bike parking.
- The feira sits on grass with the vendors hidden behind their stalls. Real feiras are on a street with tarps over the aisle, crowded with crates, and the vendors face the shoppers.

C. Buildings
- Several roofs (the north-row green and white houses, the west house) show a gray "picture frame" panel with a blob. It looks like a broken mis-cropped roof.
- The south row is pure top-down roofs while the north row is ¾ facades: a perspective clash.
- No building cast shadows on the sidewalk; facades feel pasted on.
- The academia interior: the tatame is a blue square with a yellow border and a "+", which looks like a placeholder. The floor is a mix of checker and parquet.
- Interiors float in a dark void (a diorama on a black desk).

D. Characters + labels
- A nameplate on every CPU = label soup. Show CPU names only on hover/nearby; keep players' and NPCs' names.
- Characters are small and low-contrast against the busy ground (fixing A helps a lot).
- The avatar-creator preview at 8× is crude, and the face is hidden by hair.

E. UI
- The desktop top bar has 9 big orange labeled buttons + a second row: heavy, and it eats the screen. Toasts collide with the tracker.
- The phone HUD is chaotic: stacked icon buttons, a truncated brand ("Vila Ipê · N..."), and the toast over the tracker.
- The avatar creator, sign-in and sign-up backgrounds are still the old smooth vector style (gradient sky, vector skyline, smooth silhouettes). The title-screen parrots are smooth vector birds over pixel art. Style clashes.
- The Me vê um / journal panels are fine but generic.

F. Lighting
- Noon is flat: no strong directional cast shadows, no ambient occlusion where buildings meet the ground or under trees.
- Night is the best look. Keep it.
