# Tudo Bem subscription ($10/month)

Decided by Jonny, 8 Oct 2026. Brainstorm spec only; nothing built yet.

## Why
Start bringing in money to fund development.

## Price
**$10 per month.**

## What subscribers get
1. **Founder badge**: only for subscribers. Shown beside the nameplate, separate from belts, nameplates, and stripes. **It stays for good, even after someone cancels.**
2. **Founders banner**: a wall item for the kitnet that also stays for good.
3. **Early previews**: subscribers see new areas and features (for example the Praia) before everyone else.
4. **Pets beyond parrots**: dogs and cats that follow your avatar around.
5. **Custom chat bubbles**: choose the chat bubble color and style. This changes only how the bubble looks, never what's written in it.

## Guardrails
- **Learning stays free.** No lessons, words, Escola reviews, or activities sit behind the subscription.
- **No pay-to-win.** Subscriber perks never count toward the words-learned or streak rankings, and the subscription never grants exclusive event words.
- Belts, nameplates, and stripes are untouched (stripe pace 5/10/20/40/80).
- Chat is never rewritten.
- RV/R$ stays earned only. The subscription doesn't sell in-game money.
- Family-room safe.

## Open questions
- **Conflict with the current rule that the beta is fully free with no real-money gates.** Decide whether subscriptions open during the beta (as a founders' early-supporter offer) or at launch.
- **Players under 13** need a parent's permission and a parent to pay. TB Safety should review.
- Which payment provider to use, and whether to offer a yearly price.
- What happens to pets, chat bubbles, and previews when someone cancels. The badge and banner stay; do the others go away?
- Pet art and following behavior need TB Art.
