## North star

First paint must feel like **arriving in São Paulo in autumn afternoon** — Praça canopy, padaria amber, soft sky, **a flock of Brazilian parrots crossing the frame** — then a calm email+password card that still belongs in that world.

Not: bland SaaS login, white Stripe form, Habbo candy splash, neon cyber city, generic “Latin town” postcard, cartoon macaw mascot stuck to a logo.

Feel: cinematic title-screen wow → one breath → sign-in. Guest CTA secondary. Same warmth as Padaria impressive hold.

## Sequence (visual)

1. **Title / intro beat (2–4s or skippable)**  
   - Hero: isometric Praça silhouette (fountain / trees / soft-sky `#A8C5D4`) OR Padaria storefront (awning terracotta `#C45C26`, glass-cool case) — pick **one** hero; don’t collage both equally.  
   - Preferred hero: **Praça late-afternoon** with Padaria awning readable in mid-ground (sells the world).  
   - Wordmark **Tudo Bem** + warm sun mark (existing logo OK). Tagline short PT-primary (Product/Curriculum copy).  
   - Light: soft warm key from left (palette lighting). Autumn leaves as soft particles or static canopy — subtle, not storm.  
   - **Parrot flock (required):** see section below.

2. **Sign-in / register card**  
   - Floats over the same world scene (dimmed soft veil, **not** hard black or flat white void).  
   - Card: cream-wall `#F5E6D3` fill, wood-warm or charcoal text, terracotta/mustard primary button.  
   - Fields: email, password; register secondary; optional “I am 18+” checkbox (Product).  
   - Guest “Explorar” / enter world = ghost or secondary, still warm.  
   - Errors: polite, no red screaming SaaS.  
   - Flock may continue softly behind/above the card (dimmed); never clip through form fields or steal tap targets.

3. **Idle-kick interstitial (same visual family)**  
   - Calm “Volte quando quiser” over soft Praça/Padaria still — same card language. Not a ban screen.  
   - Optional: 1–2 distant birds, not a full flyby (keep calm).

## Brazilian parrot flock (Jonny lock)

### Species / silhouette
- Mix **arara** (macaw — longer tail, larger body) + **papagaio** (parrot — stockier, shorter tail). Readable at title distance as Brazilian birds, not generic seagulls.
- Count: cinematic flock of **~6–12** birds in one pass (not a single mascot loop; not a chaotic swarm of 40).
- Path: enter off one side of sky, arc across soft-sky above Praça canopy, exit opposite — **above** wordmark / card, not through UI.
- Depth: 2–3 depth layers (far small / mid hero / near soft silhouette) for cinematic parallax. Near layer never covers CTA.
- Motion: smooth glide + slight wing beat; loopable or one-shot then idle sky. Skippable with intro skip.
- Style: same 2.5D / iso world language as Praça — painted/soft edges, not photoreal stock, not chibi stickers.

### Palette (birds)
Keep SP warmth; bird accents may be richer than UI chrome but stay Disney-safe:
| Role | Direction |
|------|-----------|
| Arara body | Deep blue / teal toward sp-green family + warm red-gold wing flash (mustard / mural-coral cousins — not neon cyan/magenta) |
| Papagaio body | Soft green canopy tones + cream/cheek warm |
| Beaks / feet | Mustard / warm wood — small accents only |
| Silhouette against soft-sky | Dark enough to read in motion; avoid pure black cutouts |

### Don’t (flock)
- Loud squawk spam / chatty SFX wall (subtle ambient OK — Product/Live Ops later)
- Birds landing on form inputs or pecking the password field (cute ≠ usable)
- Pirate / cartoon “Polly” tropes, soccer-jersey mashups, brand mashups of real zoo logos
- Neon macaw rainbow that breaks the autumn Praça grade
- Flock so dense the Praça silhouette disappears
