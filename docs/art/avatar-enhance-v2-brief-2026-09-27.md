## Hard locks (silhouette · face · proportions · outfit)

### 1. Proportions
- **~6–6.5 head heights** standing; adult SP hangout, not bobble-head Habbo.
- Slight stylization OK; **no** deep chibi, no sausage limbs, no stubby torso.
- Feet plant with warm-ink contact shadow (~30–35% opacity).
- Hats remain silhouette heroes but **sit on skull** — no floating or skull-swallow.

### 2. Silhouette
- Readable at Praça distance from hair + outfit + posture alone (before nameplate).
- Hair mass and outfit break must differ across player bases and NPCs.
- Layer order stays: **body → clothes → hair → face → hat** (create + shop equip unbroken).
- Free create (Product lock): skin / hair / face / body / pronoun + one plain starter outfit. Shop hats/clothes later — hats must still read first when equipped.

### 3. Face
- Soft shaded brows / eyes / mouth; idle friendly read at distance.
- **Fail:** pie-cut eyes, triangle nose, hard black comic outline as the whole style, blank sticker face.
- Skin: soft value bands (subsurface-ish), not flat plastic one-tone.
- Emotion: calm smile default; Carlos stays patient baker calm (not clown).

### 4. Outfit readability
- Cloth needs **≥2 value/fold bands** on jeans, tops, aprons — match Padaria material richness.
- Starter outfit: clean SP casual (tee/jeans/shoes or equivalent), cool enough you’d wear it — not Habbo primary rainbow.
- Shop pieces later must still silhouette-clear on the same body.
- Palette: stay on `palette.md` warmth (cream, terracotta, sp-green, wood-warm) — **anti-Habbo candy** (no neon magenta/cyan, no pure white sterile, no rainbow primaries as default).
