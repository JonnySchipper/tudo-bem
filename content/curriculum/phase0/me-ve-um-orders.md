# Me vê um… — tray minigame order phrases (Phase 0)

Used as audio + text tickets. Scheduled cards from padaria + numbers decks. Mistake → Seu Carlos politely repeats once.

## Level Verde (EN labels on shelves OK)

| # | PT (canonical) | EN gloss (Verde ticket) |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Me vê um pão na chapa. | I’ll take a pão na chapa (grilled buttered bread). |
| 2 | Me vê um café com leite. | I’ll take a coffee with milk. |
| 3 | Um suco de laranja, por favor. | An orange juice, please. |
| 4 | Uma água, por favor. | A water, please. |
| 5 | Me vê um pastel. | I’ll take a pastel (savory fried pastry). |
| 6 | Me vê uma coxinha. | I’ll take a coxinha (chicken croquette). |
| 7 | Dois pães na chapa. | Two pães na chapa. |
| 8 | Um café e uma água. | A coffee and a water. |

## Accept notes
- Accents optional on typed answers when free-type later; Phase 0 chips can be exact.  
- Digits OK for counts (`2` ≡ dois).  
- obrigado / obrigada both OK on thank-you beat after tray.  
- English-only orders never score 3.  
- Do **not** gloss “Me vê” as “Give me” (too blunt / classroom-wrong for padaria register).
