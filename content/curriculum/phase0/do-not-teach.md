# Do-not-teach list (DRAFT)

Purpose: nothing on this list enters the item bank, NPC lines, chips, minigame orders, chat glosses, or parrot hints — even as a “wrong answer” example that normalizes the form.

Register reminder: Brazilian Portuguese only. Default = educated informal (você, a gente, legal, tá, pra). Tag regional color `paulista` / `geral` / `praia`. Never teach European Portuguese as a target.

## 1. Alcohol & nightlife framing
- cerveja, chopp, vinho, caipirinha, dose, barzinho (as teaching targets)
- “vamos beber”, “tô chapado”, hangover talk
- Note: padaria coffee/juice/água are fine; “bar” as a place word is deferred until a Disney-safe framing exists

## 2. Dating, flirting, sensuality
- crush, ficante, ficar (romantic sense), beijo, abraço (romantic), paquera
- pickup lines, compliments on body/appearance beyond clothes-shop honesty (Nanda: hat looks good / not)
- hermoso/a romantic register; “gênero” chat for pickup

## 3. Slurs & hate
- Any racial, ethnic, LGBTQ+, disability, or regional slur (PT or EN)
- Softened spellings, asterisks, leetspeak versions of the same
- “brincadeira” framing does not unblock

## 4. Politics & religion arena
- Party names, candidates, elections, protest slogans as teachable content
- Proselytizing lines; sectarian jokes
- Safe: generic “feriado”, calendar saints’ days as date vocab only (Phase 1+)

## 5. European Portuguese forms (never target)
- tu (as default 2sg), vós
- vocês with European clitics as model
- pequeno-almoço (use café da manhã), autocarro (use ônibus), telemóvel (use celular)
- European gerund/inflected infinitive as teaching goals
- Accept-list may *tolerate* a learner typing an EU form once with a soft rephrase to BR — never reinforce it as correct

## 6. Cartoon slang dumps & classroom stiffness
- Overstacked youth slang as default path (mano every line, top/topzérrimo spam)
- Textbook-only stiffness as the only model (“Vossa Senhoria”, endless “o senhor deseja?” without warm padaria voice)
- Mixed code that teaches Spanglish as a goal (“vou order um coffee”)

## 7. Unsafe / COPPA-adjacent
- Personal address, phone, school name, home address as prompts
- Asking player age beyond the age gate flow
- Sexual content, self-harm, weapons play as vocab targets

## 8. False friends & trap “teachables” we refuse
- Teaching “esquisito” as “exquisite”
- Teaching “pasta” as Italian pasta without BR sense (folder / dough) clarified — prefer pão, pastel, coxinha for food track
- Teaching por/para via dense grammar dumps; teach via scenes only
