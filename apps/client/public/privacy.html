Privacy Policy

Effective October 9, 2026

Tudo Bem is a free browser game for learning Brazilian Portuguese, at playtudobem.com. The same game is also served at tudo-bem.fly.dev. It is operated by Tudo Bem, in Davenport, Florida, USA. This page describes the public beta as it works on the effective date.

Who this is for

The beta is for adults, 18 and older. We do not ask for a date of birth, and we do not check an ID. If you create an account with email and a password, you can tick a box that says you are 18 or older. If you tick it, we store the time you did. Continue with Google does not show that box. The game is still meant for adults.

Accounts

You can sign in two ways.

Email and password. We store the email address you type and a scrambled form of your password (a hash). We can check the password later. We cannot turn the hash back into the password, and we do not store the password itself.

Continue with Google. The button loads Google’s sign-in script from accounts.google.com. Google checks your account and sends us a token. From that token we keep the verified email address and a Google account identifier, so we can recognize the same Google account next time. We do not store your Google profile name or photo. The name other players see is the character name you choose in the game.

We also store when the account was created and when you last signed in. If you already have an email account and later use Continue with Google with that same email, we link the Google identifier to the existing account. From then on that account signs in with Google only: the old password stops working, and devices that were signed in with it are signed out.

In the game, the Conta (Account) panel in the settings menu lets you change your password, sign out on every device, download your data, and delete your account.

What the game saves

On the server, tied to your account, we save:

  • The character you make: name, pronoun, and look
  • Play progress: in-game coins (called RV), hats, furniture and your apartment, a parrot, academy and bakery progress, errands, a word notebook, a stamp card, friendships with characters, language-diary words, and pictures you take with the in-game camera (shots of the virtual neighborhood)
  • A short Portuguese summary of a recent conversation with a character, when that feature has something to remember. The summary is not a copy of the chat
  • Your friends list: the other players you added as friends

Ordinary chat in a place is shown to the people who are there with you. We do not save a full transcript of that chat on your profile.

Safety, reports, and feedback

Chat, and some lines you type to characters, pass through a safety filter that runs on our server. If a message is warned, blocked, or sent for review, we keep a log of it: your player id, character name, the place, the text, and the reason. The same log stores reports you file about another player.

You can send a feedback note from the game. We save the note, the category if you pick one, the place you were in, and a contact (an email or a nickname) only if you type one. If you are signed in, we also save your account id and player id with the note instead of a contact, so we can match it to your account. We never save your email address with a note.

We keep the safety log and feedback notes until we have reviewed them and no longer need them. There is no automatic deletion yet. Deleting your account removes the entries we can match to it.

AI conversations

Some conversations with characters are written by an AI model from xAI (api.x.ai), when that service is configured on the server. For those turns we send xAI the text of the conversation — what you typed and what the character said — together with instructions for the model, so it can reply. The instructions include your character name, the pronoun you picked, and the saved summary of earlier conversations with that character, if there is one. At the end of a conversation we may also ask xAI for a short summary.

The full transcript of that conversation stays in the server’s memory only while it is recent. It expires within a few hours and is not written into your saved profile. What we keep is the short summary. We drop a summary that repeats a stretch of your own words.

If xAI is not available, the game uses written dialogue and does not send the text.

Cookies and storage on your device

Signing in sets a cookie named tb_session. Page scripts cannot read it (it is HttpOnly). It is sent only to our site (SameSite=Lax). Over HTTPS it is also marked Secure. It lasts about 30 days and is refreshed while you keep using the game. It is how the server knows this browser is signed in. Logging out clears it.

The browser’s local storage also holds a few things so the game can pick up where you left off: a profile token, a small record of the signed-in email, and settings such as sound, music, and whether English hints are on. Session storage remembers that you already passed the title screen and the last room you were in.

If you play a solo copy with no server, the character can be stored only on that device.

We do not use advertising cookies or analytics cookies.

IP addresses. The server uses your IP address to limit repeated sign-in attempts, signups, and feedback notes. Those counters live in the server’s memory, drop old entries after at most an hour, and are not written to the database. A wrong admin password is written to the server log with the IP address it came from. Our host keeps server logs only for a short time.

The game page loads typefaces from Google Fonts (fonts.googleapis.com and fonts.gstatic.com).

Where it is stored

The game server and its database run on Fly.io, in the United States. The database is backed up every hour onto the same server storage. We keep the 48 most recent backups, so a backup is replaced after about 48 hours of the server running.

Who else sees information

  • Other players in the same place see your character name, look, and chat.
  • Leaderboards in the game show character names and scores (words learned, study streak) to every player, not only the people in the same place.
  • Your friends see when you are online and which place you are in.
  • Fly.io hosts the server and the database, as described above.
  • Google processes Continue with Google, as described above.
  • xAI processes conversation text when the AI conversation feature is on, as described above.

We do not sell personal information.

Your data and deletion

Download. In the game, open the settings menu, then Conta (Account), then Baixar meus dados (Download my data). You get a JSON file with your account details (not the password hash) and everything saved for your character.

Delete. In the same panel, Apagar conta (Delete account) asks for your password, or, for an account that signs in with Google, for the account email typed out. Deleting removes the account and its sessions, your character and its progress, your pictures, academies and bakeries you founded, your place in other players’ friends lists and academies, the feedback notes saved with your account, and safety-log entries filed by you or about you. It happens at once and cannot be undone. Copies in the hourly backups are replaced within about 48 hours of the server running, as described above.

If you cannot sign in, email team@playtudobem.com from the address on the account, or tell us that address, and we will delete it for you.
